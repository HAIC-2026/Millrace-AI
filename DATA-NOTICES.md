## Terms and limitations

The [State portal terms](https://portal.ehawaii.gov/page/terms-of-use/) state that automated access, including scripts, is prohibited and restrict commercial use or resale of data derived from its services unless separately authorized in writing. The older [State terms page](https://hawaii.gov/soh-terms-of-use/) describes an informational-use permission for unmodified documents and excludes page design/layout; it is not a blanket permission to publish modified extracts or a reason to disregard the portal terms. These terms apply to access/use as stated and raise a concrete unresolved commercial-use limitation for State-derived content. The projection is prepared for informational review; it is not a representation of unrestricted commercial rights. Seek a specific grant or replace/rederive from an independently authorized source before a commercial use covered by those terms. A direct Census Data API reconstruction of the ACS figures is a potential alternative, but identical values, definitions, and all 78 geographies have not been demonstrated here. Other State administrative figures and program facts would need their own independent replacements or permission.

The [Census API terms](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/about/terms-of-service.html) apply to the Census API metadata in this packet. Display prominently: “This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.” Do not use the data to identify respondents; do not represent modified content as unmodified Census content. Attribute derived calculations to their author and identify Census as the underlying source, consistent with [Census citation guidance](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/citation.html). [17 USC §105](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t17/s105/) and [Census policy DS027](https://www2.census.gov/foia/ds_policies/ds027.pdf) distinguish federal employee works from privately authored material. [17 USC §102](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t17/s102/) describes the scope of protected expression; that does not eliminate contract terms or grant rights to a source compilation. Software licensing does not apply a blanket license to outside data.
