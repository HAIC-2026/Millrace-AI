District Brief Builder

Ask about a Hawaiʻi House or Senate district, check the evidence and hand off a one-page brief. Figures come from bundled public data; nothing is fetched live.

About this app, its data and privacy

District Brief Builder is a working prototype by team Millrace AI for the HAIC 2026 Know Your District challenge. It helps legislative staff turn a district question into a sourced, one-page brief for agency follow-up, using published aggregate data. It is not an official State of Hawaiʻi service. Agencies decide eligibility and current availability.

Try an example

The page opens on House 028, Older-adult support at home, with its plan already run. Inspect a finding’s source, then choose Create recommended brief: it builds a real one-page brief with the Kūpuna Care agency action, ready to print or save as a PDF.

Data and dates

ACS estimates show their period, universe and 90% margin of error. Administrative counts show their period, coverage and source location. Program references show their source and checked date. Sources were retrieved October 30, 2026.

Statistics are ACS 2020–2024 estimates for the 2024 statistical legislative districts. The 2024 or 2026 map setting changes the boundary drawn, not the statistics. In the current data the 2024 and 2026 boundaries match for all 76 districts.

Where your work goes

Plans, selections, typed requests and brief edits are sent to this app’s server to calculate results and render the brief. The app does not save or log them. Case files are built and read in your browser; running or reopening a case sends its plan and selections to the server. Data catalog filters and search text are sent to this app’s server in the page request, not to OpenAI. The hosting services (Cloudflare, a Caddy proxy and AWS) can keep connection logs, including IP addresses and requested addresses, which can contain catalog search text.

Contact

Questions or corrections: open an issue at github.com/HAIC-2026/Millrace-AI/issues (opens in a new tab; posting requires GitHub sign-in). Do not include resident or confidential details. Team Millrace AI maintains this competition prototype. No agency ownership or service level is claimed.

What is sent and how a question runs

With your question the app sends public plan context (the confirmed and draft plan), value-free catalog identifiers and labels, and references to selected findings. The app does not add staff notes, computed figures, cookies or case identifiers, but anything you type in the question is sent. The request asks OpenAI not to store the response; that is not a zero-retention guarantee, and OpenAI’s own policies apply. The server checks each proposal against the allowed operations and sources before using it.

Ask a question (AI) is turned off. Nothing is sent to OpenAI. Use Advanced investigation or a typed request; the findings, brief, sources and case files work the same.
